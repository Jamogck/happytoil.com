## What it is

A tool for keeping grazing records that are worth keeping: which paddock, how many
animals, how long, what the grass looked like going in and coming out — recorded quickly
enough in the field that it actually gets done.

## Why it exists

Graziers already collect most of this in their heads. The value isn’t in the collecting,
it’s in being able to look back two seasons and see that a particular paddock has been
grazed too early three springs running, or that the rest period everyone assumes is
thirty days is closer to nineteen.

Most grazing software either wants to be a full farm ERP or is a notebook with a login.
The interesting space is in between: a small number of measurements, entered fast, turned
into a handful of questions the manager was already asking.

## What I noticed

That the people with the best pastures were the ones with the longest memory of them —
and that memory is exactly the thing a small tool can extend.
