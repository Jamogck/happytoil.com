## What it is

A plain reference site for raising broilers on pasture: feed conversion, processing
yields, shelter and stocking figures, and the cost arithmetic that decides whether a batch
is worth doing at all.

## Why it exists

The information exists, but it’s scattered across forums, extension PDFs from three
decades ago, and the comment sections of videos. Someone raising their first hundred birds
has to assemble it themselves, usually while the birds are already growing.

## What I noticed

That nearly every question a beginner asks is really a question about a number — how much
feed, how many square feet, what it costs per bird, what it sells for — and that the
answers are stable enough to write down once and keep.
