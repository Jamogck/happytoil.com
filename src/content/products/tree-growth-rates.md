## What it is

A reference for species growth rates: how much height and diameter to expect per year,
under what conditions, and how long until a tree is doing the thing you planted it to do —
shade, screen, timber, fruit, windbreak.

## Why it exists

Nursery tags say "fast growing." Extension publications give ranges without context.
Anyone planning a windbreak, a woodlot, or a row of shade trees is making a thirty-year
decision on the basis of an adjective.

## What I noticed

That a question with a real answer was being met everywhere with vague ones — and that
this happens constantly in agriculture, which is most of why the products on this page
exist.
