Bring us in early

We put together pre-construction budgets while a project is still in design, then do the shop drawings, build it, finish it and install it with our own crews. Solid surface tops and upholstery are in-house too.

Karn is FSC certified, so our work can count toward LEED credits. We're a licensed Class A fine carpentry subcontractor in Virginia and a SWaM certified small, veteran-owned business.

Budget or bid
