About Explus

About 60 technicians, artisans and craftspeople work out of our Sterling production shop. We've built museum display cases for more than 30 years, from our Archive Standard Series to custom cases engineered around a single artifact, with UV-filtering glazing and low-VOC materials.

Bring us in during design and we'll help value-engineer the exhibit before it goes to bid. We also offer 3D printing (FDM and SLA) for models, mounts and parts.

  • Estimating and value engineering
  • Project management
  • Exhibit detailing
  • Scenic production
  • Graphics and art production
  • Metalworking
  • AV, electrical and lighting
  • Electro-mechanical interactives
  • Artifact mounting
