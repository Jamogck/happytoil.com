Since 1950

Seventy-plus years. One Virginia Beach shop.

Founded by Paul Gallup on Birdneck Road as a small custom millwork company, Premier was purchased by George R. Melnyk, Sr. in 1974 and moved to Viking Drive in 1984 to meet growing commercial demand. Today it's led by George R. Melnyk, Jr., with a senior team whose tenure runs decades deep.
