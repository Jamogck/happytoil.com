A family-run shop on Viking Drive

Paul Gallup started Premier on Birdneck Road in 1950 as a small custom millwork company. George R. Melnyk, Sr. bought it in 1974 and moved it to Viking Drive in 1984 to keep up with commercial work. George R. Melnyk, Jr. runs it today, with more than 50 people on staff and senior team members who have been here 20 to 30 years.
